Nexo Preview Update 6 audio

Seven UI cues edited from four Adobe Firefly sound-effect variations generated
on 2026-11-15 through the project owner's signed-in Firefly account.

The exact prompt, original file hashes, variation mapping, output hashes and
processing are recorded in sources.json. These are generated audio assets;
they are not the Kenney samples used by earlier Nexo previews. This project
does not identify the Firefly outputs as CC0 or as an open-source sound pack.

The browser offered one free batch containing four one-second variations.
Further generation showed the daily quota/upgrade notice. The seven cues are
edited derivatives of that batch, not seven independent Firefly generations.

Edits: trim, playback-rate adjustment, reverse for Back, mono conversion,
high/low-pass filtering, fade-in/out, gain matching and Vorbis encoding.
No synthesized waveforms, new model-generated audio or old pack samples were
mixed into these derivatives. Raw generated WAVs are preserved separately.
